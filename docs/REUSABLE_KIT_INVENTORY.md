## Master structure

- 1 Master Director
- Coordinators (1 per team or shared)
- 5 parallel teams, same architecture, different locations

## Event phases (every activation)

1. **Pre-event** — scout, police/force courtesy + written approval, flyer print, briefing, food, truck
2. **Event** — setup, cooking demo + music + dancers, egg distribute/redistribute, flyer + QR, live video
3. **Post-event** — pack reusable materials, debrief, upload media, count installs/sign-ups, settle day wages, document learnings

## Approvals (non-negotiable)

- Courtesy visit to police and relevant forces
- Written or documented approval before public activation
- Keep copies in team folder / campaign tracker

## Cost discipline

- Prefer hire for truck; buy once for banners, cooker, sound
- No single-use décor
- Target: activation cost low enough to repeat weekly across LGAs

## Physical neural network

Eggs move across locations and agents (people + trucks + kiosks) so supply meets demand in real time. Software tracks; the network is physical.
