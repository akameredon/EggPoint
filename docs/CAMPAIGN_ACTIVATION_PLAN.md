## 2. Physical Activation Architecture (5 Identical Teams)

Every activation day uses **exactly the same architecture** so it is cheap to repeat and materials stay reusable.

### Per Team Kit (must be reusable)
- 1 truck (or hired for the day)
- Egg cooker station (demo cooking)
- Music system + speakers
- 4–6 dancers / energy crew
- Food for workers (2 square meals: morning + after work)
- Flyer stock with QR code → app install + simple “make money” steps
- Branded reusable banners / flags / aprons (no single-use waste)
- Media person (phone or camera) for continuous coverage

### Master Structure
- 1 Master Director (overall)
- Team of Coordinators (one per team or shared)
- 5 parallel teams covering different locations on the same day or staggered

### Event Phases (every activation)
1. **Pre-event** – location scouting, police/force courtesy visits, written approvals, flyer printing, team briefing, food procurement, truck booking
2. **Event** – arrival, setup, cooking demo + music + dancers, egg distribution / redistribution, flyer + QR conversion, live video coverage
3. **Post-event** – pack reusable materials, debrief, upload media, count installs / sign-ups, settle any day wages, document learnings

### Approvals
- Always pay courtesy to police and all relevant forces.
- Collect written or documented approval before any public activation.
- Never skip this. It is non-negotiable.

### Physical Neural Network
Eggs are distributed and redistributed across locations and agents so that supply and demand meet in real time. The “network” is physical people + trucks + kiosks/agents moving stock, not only software.

## 3. Flyer + QR Conversion Loop

Flyer must contain:
- Strong visual + ₦15 claim
- QR code that lands on a dedicated install / signup page
- Extremely simple steps to make money:

  1. Install the EggPoint app
  2. Sign up (as yourself or as a buyer you introduce)
  3. Share your referral link / code
  4. Earn when the buyer you brought completes their first order / verification

This is the core growth loop: street → flyer → install → referral → more buyers → more street demand.

## 6. Team Compensation Psychology (Salary + Shares from Net Profit Pool)

### Rules
- Anyone joining the team first states the salary they want **and** the share percentage they want.
- After one month of work they answer (honest survey style):
  > “Do you believe the service you rendered is commensurate with the salary + shares you chose?”
- At month-end:
  - All expenses are deducted.
  - Net profit is calculated.
  - The net profit becomes the **pool**.
  - Everyone who declared salary + shares does the maths together.
  - If the pool can cover the declared salaries + the share value, they are paid.
  - If not, the shortfall is shared (they bear the loss proportionally). This trains them to increase output the next month.
- Result: people only earn from real net profit created by the phones / activity available. More activity → bigger pool → higher personal outcome. Psychology game that forces focus and ownership.

Founder also sits on the same salary + share rules. Company profit does not automatically become founder net worth.

## 8. Capital Strategy (Small Pledges → Liquidity)

Do not only chase big investors first.

Also approach friends who have ₦10k / ₦20k / ₦50k / ₦100k.

Method:
- Ask them to project their net worth in 20 years.
- Ask them to commit **10% of that future net worth** as investment capital into EggPoint.
- They do **not** pay the full amount day one.
- The pledged amount is spread over 12–24 months.
- Earnings / ownership are calculated against the full pledged amount even while they are still paying in tranches.
- This creates available working capital / “phones to work” without waiting only for large cheques.
